# Monitoring Suggestions

Practical ways to stay abreast of Australian peptide regulatory developments beyond the repo's daily automation.

## Search Alerts

- **Google Alerts** — Set up email digests for phrases such as:
  - `TGA peptide`
  - `unapproved peptide Australia`
  - `peptide inquiry`
  - https://www.google.com/alerts

- **TGA Safety Alerts RSS feed** — Subscribe directly to the TGA's safety alerts feed for real-time advisory notifications.
  - https://www.tga.gov.au/safety/safety-monitoring-and-information/safety-alerts (RSS link on page)

## Industry Publications

- **Pharmacy Daily** — Industry-side coverage of TGA alerts, PBS changes, and pharmacy practice developments that mainstream news often misses.
  - https://pharmacydaily.com.au/

- **Australian Pharmacist** — Feature articles on compounding, regulatory guidance, and professional standards.
  - https://www.australianpharmacist.com.au/

## Regulator Email Alerts

- **Ahpra news** — Email alerts for new guidance, regulatory actions, and newsletters.
  - https://www.ahpra.gov.au/News

- **Medical Board of Australia newsletters** — Monthly newsletters covering prescribing guidance and professional standards.
  - https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/News/Newsletters

- **NSW Health media releases** — Subscribe for ministerial and departmental announcements, including safety information bulletins.
  - https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases

- **Victoria Health media releases** — Subscribe for Victorian Department of Health announcements.
  - https://www.health.vic.gov.au/media-centre

## Social Media

- **X (Twitter) follows** — Official and journalist accounts:
  - TGA (@TGAMedia or search TGA)
  - Ahpra (@Ahpra)
  - Henrietta Cook (@HenriettaCook) — The Age journalist who broke the Victorian anaphylaxis cluster story
  - Others covering health policy and regulation as relevant

- **Private X list** — Create a private list containing the above accounts for one-tap scanning of peptide-related posts.
  - https://help.x.com/en/using-x/lists-on-x

## Parliamentary Inquiry

- **House of Representatives Standing Committee on Health, Aged Care and Disability email updates** — Subscribe for hearing schedules, submission deadlines, witness lists, and report releases once the peptide inquiry is formally listed.
  - https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/House/Health_Aged_Care_and_Disability

*These complement the daily Grok automation and the dated/research source files in this repo. Review quarterly to add or retire sources.*
