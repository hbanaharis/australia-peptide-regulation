# Research & Reference Sources

Ongoing monitoring sources beyond dated news items. These should be checked by the daily automation alongside news searches.

## Peer-Reviewed Literature & Trials

- **PubMed** — Search for peer-reviewed peptide safety, efficacy, and adverse event literature. Useful queries: `BPC-157`, `retatrutide`, `CJC-1295`, `ipamorelin`, `TB-500`, `GHK-Cu`, `melanotan`.
  - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/

- **ClinicalTrials.gov** — US and international clinical trial registrations for peptides (e.g., retatrutide trials).
  - https://clinicaltrials.gov/

- **ANZCTR (Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry)** — Australian trial registrations relevant to peptide therapeutics.
  - https://www.anzctr.org.au/

## Parliamentary & Legislative

- **House of Representatives Standing Committee on Health, Aged Care and Disability** — Formal terms of reference, submissions, and hearing transcripts for the peptide inquiry once published.
  - https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/House/Health_Aged_Care_and_Disability

- **AustLII** — Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 (Cth) and associated case law on unlawful advertising, importation, and supply of therapeutic goods.
  - http://www.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdb/au/legis/cth/consol_act/tga1989191/

- **Federal Register of Legislation** — Current text of the Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 and Therapeutic Goods Regulations 1990, including Schedule 8 compounding exemptions.
  - https://www.legislation.gov.au/

## Consumer Protection & Advertising

- **ACCC (Australian Competition and Consumer Commission)** — Consumer protection actions, misleading advertising investigations, and ACL enforcement relevant to peptide marketing.
  - https://www.accc.gov.au/

## Sport & Anti-Doping

- **Sport Integrity Australia** — Australian anti-doping policy, athlete advisories, and testing outcomes related to peptides.
  - https://www.sportintegrity.gov.au/

- **WADA Prohibited List** — Global anti-doping status of peptides (e.g., BPC-157 remains prohibited).
  - https://www.wada-ama.org/en/prohibited-list

## Coronial

- **Coroners Court of Victoria / relevant state coroners** — Inquest findings into deaths linked to unapproved peptide use, including the Richard Norton inquest (reported ~31 October 2026).
  - https://www.coronerscourt.vic.gov.au/

## International Comparators

- **US FDA** — FDA compounding policies, the 503A/503B framework, peptide compounding enforcement, and the HHS October 2026 notice on BPC-157, TB-500, Epitalon, Semax, KPV, MOTS-c.
  - https://www.fda.gov/

- **EMA (European Medicines Agency)** — European regulatory position on peptides and compounding.
  - https://www.ema.europa.eu/

- **MHRA (UK Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency)** — UK regulatory position on peptides.
  - https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/medicines-and-healthcare-products-regulatory-agency

## Independent Trackers

- **Peptide Register (Australia)** — Independent tracker of 22 peptides, 40 prescription providers, 52 observed suppliers in Australia; TGA scheduling and enforcement trends per peptide. Updated November 2026.
  - https://www.peptideregister.com/
