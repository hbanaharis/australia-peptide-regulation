# Update — 27 November 2026

The House of Representatives Standing Committee on Health, Aged Care and Disability has formally commenced its inquiry into the safety and quality of prescribing peptides in Australia.

Key details from the Committee’s media release (issue date Thursday, 26 November 2026):
- Inquiry examines the prevalence and patterns of peptide prescribing and use, and the safety and quality of prescribing practices involving products available through online prescribing services.
- Committee Chair Dr Mike Freelander MP noted that online models of care can improve convenience and access but must be accompanied by appropriate patient safeguards. The Committee will look at what is driving increasing consumer interest, the role of advertising, social media and digital platforms, and whether the existing regulatory framework remains appropriate as these models evolve.
- Submissions are invited from government departments, patients and consumers, healthcare practitioners, professional and regulatory bodies, researchers, online healthcare providers and other interested parties. **Submissions are open until 29 January 2027.**
- Full terms of reference and submission details are on the Committee’s website.

This follows Health Minister Mark Butler’s request (letter of 20 November 2026) for an urgent inquiry into online peptide prescribing practices.
